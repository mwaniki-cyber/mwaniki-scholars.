## Reporting a Vulnerability

We take security vulnerabilities seriously. If you discover a security issue
in this project, please report it privately.

### How to Report

Send an email to owenquincy69@gmail.com.

> ⚠️ Do NOT report security issues in the public issue tracker, forums, or
> pull requests — that would expose the vulnerability to others before a fix
> is available.

Please include as much of the following as possible:

- **Description** — what the vulnerability is and how it works
- **Impact** — potential harm or scope
- **Affected versions** — which releases are vulnerable
- **Steps to reproduce** — a minimal proof-of-concept
- **Suggested fix** — if you have a patch or mitigation
- **Name/handle** — if you'd like credit in the disclosure

### What to Expect

- We acknowledge your report **within 48 hours**.
- Within **5 business days**, we assess the issue and determine severity.
- We send status updates at least every **7 days** until resolution.

### Resolution Outcomes

- **Accepted:** We develop and release a fix, coordinate disclosure on an
  agreed date, and credit you (with your consent).
- **Declined:** We explain why the report isn't considered a vulnerability
  and thank you for your effort.

### Responsible Disclosure

Please **do not publicly disclose** the vulnerability until we've had a
reasonable opportunity (typically **90 days**) to investigate and ship a fix.
