Community Rules

Please read and agree to these rules before entering the community or General Chat.

1
Respect everyone

Treat students, tutors and other members respectfully. Harassment, bullying and personal attacks are not allowed.

2
Keep discussions academic

Use community spaces for learning, academic discussion and appropriate social interaction.

3
No spam

Do not flood channels with repeated, irrelevant or disruptive messages.

4
Protect privacy

Do not publish another person's private information, credentials or personal documents.

5
Share appropriate material

Photos, documents, GIFs and other shared material must be appropriate for an academic community.

6
Follow community instructions

Community moderators and tutors may remove disruptive content or restrict access when necessary.
